Pro Tips for Students

1

Be Specific with Prompts

Instead of "Help with history," try "Explain the causes of World War I for a high school essay with three main points."

2

Ask for Explanations

Always ask "Why?" and "Can you explain that differently?" to deepen your understanding.

3

Use It to Check Your Work

Paste your essay and ask "What can I improve?" or "Is my argument logical?"

4

Create Custom Study Materials

Ask ChatGPT to generate practice questions, flashcards, or quizzes based on your textbook.

5

Learn by Teaching

Explain a concept to ChatGPT and ask it to point out any mistakes in your understanding.

6

Maintain Academic Integrity

Use ChatGPT to learn and understand, not to copy. Always write in your own words and cite sources.
